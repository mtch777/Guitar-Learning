What this logic covers
A call is an anchor-only attack or burst; the response is the contrasting material before the next return. Notes played together, unknown pitches, sustained overlap and unclosed endings remain explicit. The scope follows source sections and anchor changes—it does not establish musical phrase boundaries.
These examples illustrate breadth within this corpus. They do not establish genre-wide accuracy or prescribe how an artist should compose.
