The 18 Sweet discrepancies are explained by **20 explicitly marked grace beats**: 16 affected bars have one notated eighth-note grace beat; two have two. The previous converter added those notation values to metrical time. The canonical extractor now keeps `notated_duration_q`, zero grace `duration_q`, exact metrical onset, source IDs, raw XML and OnBeat/BeforeBeat type separately. It does not fabricate grace performance duration. Empty voice slots retain indices; empty bars receive explicit MusicXML silence without inventing source-note occurrences. MusicXML uses constant exact divisions and preserves source key signatures.

Independent alphaTab notation-time comparison: Cloud 3,563 beats / 5,314 notes, zero mismatches; Sweet 5,958 beats / 10,138 notes, zero unexplained mismatches. Sweet has **three explicit harmonic-technique differences between GP stored pitch and alphaTab sounding pitch**; these remain reported and are not silently overwritten. Techniques remain in canonical GP metadata, not all in inference features. Partitura merges some tie continuations in its model note array; written-note audits use unmerged occurrences.

Reproduce from repository root (Python dependencies in `.venv`):

Generated with seed 17 and separately fitted track models. Each output has 16 explicitly requested bars. Track choices: Cloud harmony 2, lead 0, bass 4; Sweet harmony 4, lead 3, bass 7. Native source tuning **and MIDI program** are preserved for selected roles. No Cloud shell is used for Sweet.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Sweet notes by track | 40 | 265 | 265 + 138 | 265 + 176 + 103 |

All eight primary files in `local-artifacts/{cloud,sweet}-native-ablations/` independently passed alphaTab expected pitch, notation timing, tuning, MIDI program, track/bar checks and MIDI export. Four stages share their generated harmonic plan; the final stage adds bass and motif recurrence. All source material remains local. This is a **limited statistical baseline**: empirical voicing-anchor coordination is not harmonic-function inference; repeated-bar periods (Cloud 2, Sweet 1) are not validated musical phrases; drums, vocals, original techniques and learned sustain handling are unfinished. Do not equate more notes with better music.

Two cross-song folds were executed: train only Cloud/hold out Sweet, then reverse. SHA leakage guards passed. Exact transposition-relative eight-event copying against the held-out song was zero in all stages. Against training, all measured shares were zero except Sweet final stage **1.3746%**. These limited window checks do not establish originality. Reports include per-track note/polyphony/sustain proxies/leaps/gaps/rhythm histograms and descriptive training/holdout distribution distances; no harmonic accuracy or perceptual preference labels exist.

Blind-review folders `local-artifacts/{cloud,sweet}-native-listening/` contain the four actual GP outputs plus an unchanged V2 baseline, alphaTab MIDI exports, anonymous synthetic audio, private answer keys and blank CSVs. **No listening judgments collected**. Sine-MIDI previews are not Guitar Pro soundbanks.
