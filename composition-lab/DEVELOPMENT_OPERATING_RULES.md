## The decision that governs the work

The goal is to understand musical relationships well enough to recreate and
vary music through the SAME relationships. Preservation is necessary. A safe edit,
an impressive representation or many tests is not sufficient musical progress.

Before substantial implementation, answer: **What can a musician understand or
do after this change that they cannot understand or do now? What observation
would show that our proposed approach is wrong?**

Source songs test general logic. They are not demonstrations to patch until they
look successful. A failed experiment can change our design; it cannot be erased
by changing the meaning of success after seeing its result.

## Operating rules

| Rule | What changes in our work | What prevents the old failure |
|---|---|---|
| Start with a musical capability | Describe the musical relationship and the intended useful change before choosing files, schemas or helpers. Include why existing relationships cannot already provide it. | No feature justified only by another field, control, report or test count. Infrastructure work names the capability or correctness failure it unlocks. |
| Test the premise cheaply | Inspect a real source case and run the smallest experiment that could refute the proposed relationship or show that the locks leave no meaningful choice. Keep a contrasting or ambiguous case. | Do not build a selector, broad harness or UI around a design already shown to admit no changed outcome. A bounded negative study remains legitimate research. |
| Justify each constraint | Mark every lock as an explicit user requirement, a necessary dependency, or a provisional experiment assumption. Identify both the released parameter and its allowed consequences. | A convenient assumption cannot quietly redefine a melody lever as a fixed-endpoint middle-note lever. Do not loosen actual requirements to force an example. |
| Make interpretation operational | State which recognition, prediction, explanation or change becomes possible because of the musical relationship. Compare with simple copying or transposition when making a transfer claim. | Reconstructing the original or attaching a chord/role label alone cannot establish useful abstraction. Keep ambiguity explicit. |
| Separate observations from decisions | Original observations and their provenance are immutable. The current composition and its context can change. Distinguish these inside the declared tier input and ownership model. | Generated output cannot become another original observation. No external source lookup, hidden answer cache or duplicate raw score may repair this distinction. |
| Design for use in sequence | Test edit A followed by B, shared dependencies, relevant owner combinations and unsupported requests at nonzero settings. Independent edits should not depend accidentally on their order. | A control is not integrated because its isolated or zero-only test passes. Legitimate context-dependent order effects must be explained, not suppressed. |
| Prefer a common musical relationship over another special path | Extend an accepted owner/equation when it represents the concept correctly. Keep operation-specific assumptions explicit. Use common export, inspection and verification paths where applicable. | No song-ID branches, manually chosen musical answers, duplicate inverse engines or per-example exceptions. Schema-version special cases must not silently disable otherwise supported owners. |
| Test claims independently | Planner first challenges the musical contract using requirements and source evidence. The implementation check then inspects actual outputs, complete locks and adversarial cases. | An independent checker that shares our admission assumptions may verify implementation while missing a flawed musical design. State what is actually independent. |
| Listen at the first verified audio | As soon as a new musical capability produces verified audio, present short reference/original/changed clips with full-band context and identical playback settings. Explain what each preserves and changes, with all three QA statuses. Routine accepted variants do not wait for approval; new uncertain musical judgments and tier exits retain their declared listening requirement. | Do not wait for the whole melody system, tier or project. Bounded correctness work can continue; repeated audits cannot indefinitely postpone this checkpoint. Technical checks do not replace the user's judgment of identity, ending, register or desired relationships. |
| Preserve meaning across updates | Compare untouched authoritative relationships, owners and obligations against the input, and test that their dependencies still respond after an edit. | Identical reconstructed notes and standalone validity cannot detect a dependency silently replaced by an absolute value. Constructors are not update operations without an explicit preservation contract. |
| Measure useful reach | Report how many source cases were examined, eligible, offered distinct alternatives, changed, were rendered, and received musical acceptance. Keep selected demonstrations separate from population measurements. | Random draws, matching pairs and passing assertions are not independent musical opportunities or a musical-quality percentage. |
| Change course when evidence warrants it | At the midpoint or roughly ten substantive working steps, compare actual capability with the promised capability and identify the cheapest decisive next experiment. | Do not repeat an unsuccessful study with more qualifiers, lower the acceptance bar, or add unrelated knobs to disguise a blocked objective. |

Explicit revert is separate.50% is reproducible release probability, not half the
notes/audible difference;100% removes direct original-value preference, not forced

## Planner's job and my responsibility

- **Start:** Planner tries to disprove the proposed capability, finds unjustified
  locks and tests whether the planned evidence would distinguish success from a
  convenient demonstration. Root owns the design decision and records objections.
- **Middle / about ten working steps:** Planner examines actual artifacts and the
  strongest counterexample. We ask whether this is still the fastest route to
  meaningful progress without sacrificing accuracy. A mid-course failed premise
  returns us to design before further scaling or presentation work.
- **End:** Planner independently checks the claims, scope, unresolved failures,
  all three QA results and actual review output. Approval names the demonstrated
  capability; it does not stand in for a user's listening judgment.

Both roles missed important failures previously. Consultation itself is not QA.
Neither reviewer agreement nor a written rule is proof that the code complies.

## One compact contract for each substantive change

Add this to the existing work item's progress record; do not create a parallel
roadmap. Fill it before substantial implementation, then update with results.

| Item | Required record |
|---|---|
| User capability | One plain-English before/after; owning tier and requirement. |
| Musical relationship | What is understood, why it matters and what remains uncertain. |
| Input / inverse | Sole preceding-tier input, owners and shared forward/backward equations. |
| Original / edited | Immutable observation evidence versus current realization and context. |
| Freedom / locks | Released choices, dependent consequences, and justification for each lock. |
| Cheap disproof | Real example, counterexample, baseline if relevant, bounded next experiment and a result that would require revising the design. |
| Integration | Relevant nonzero combinations, successive edits, shared dependencies and expected refusals. |
| Three QA gates | Fresh frozen song; stripped-input exact inverse; meaningful musical abstraction. Separate statuses and evidence. |
| Musical reach | Full opportunity denominator versus chosen example; actual changes, rendering and scoped listening status. |
| Decision | Continue, revise or reject this approach; Planner objections, root rationale and next action. |

The fresh random song remains frozen before analysis. Keep its first failure.
After a repair, a replay is development evidence, not retroactive unseen success.
Preserve accepted detectors, exact timing/identity/techniques and old baselines.
Run targeted checks during iteration and the required regressions before delivery;
do not repeatedly expand a full suite without a relevant unresolved risk.
