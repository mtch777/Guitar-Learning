## New physical relationship, with explicit limits

For a complete simultaneous target hand, derive required frets from existing
owned pitches/strings/settings. A contact is a positive integral fret with a
contiguous string interval. A one-string interval is a point contact; multiple
strings at the same fret are a possible barre. No invented sounding notes.

For each string, the effective fret is the maximum fret among contacts covering
it, or zero when uncovered. It must equal that string's required fret exactly.
Thus a lower barre may lie under a higher contact, but cannot cross a required
open or lower-fret string. In the initial scope, a barre may cross only actually
played contiguous strings: no unobserved muting/picking of intervening strings.
Retain same-part overlap/occupied-string guards across the full written span.

Enumerate contacts generically from required positive fret values and eligible
contiguous string intervals; enumerate complete covers under an explicit model
of at most four distinct fretting digits, excluding thumb use. One contact uses
one digit. This is a bounded model assumption, not an anatomical theorem or a
universal exclusion of other guitar techniques. Retain all minimal covers, or a
complete feasible-cover structure plus a serialization witness. Do not choose a
particular player's fingering or claim an optimum performer technique.

Every candidate cover must emerge from generic enumeration; independent
checking must not encode a source song or a particular shape as a special case.

Contact coverage establishes only simultaneous nominal contact consistency.
It does NOT prove finger reach, joint posture, pressure, clean tone, barre
comfort, muting, movement from the preceding hand, release into the next hand,
or acoustic duration. Preserve/declare those unknowns. Do not invent a fret-span
threshold, reuse observed source maxima as anatomy, or call this equivalent to
the stricter observed-whole-hand evidence.

Contacts are a checked feasibility witness derived from existing musical and
physical owners, not another editable pitch/fret stream. The inverse does not
need a hidden corpus or contact cache to recover exact zero. No new GP fingering
annotations are added: existing source annotations remain locked.

## Policy boundary and postconditions

The only replacement is the old exact-whole-geometry witness predicate. It is
replaced by a strict contact-model proof with `human_playability=UNVERIFIED`.
Run every subsequent source, motion-uncertainty, tie/execution, technique-class
and approved-pedal check that previously lay after the geometry refusal. A
contact pass cannot short-circuit them. Keep old default controls/results intact.

The provisional status is mandatory in serialized decisions, private GP/audio
artifact metadata and the review presentation. Do not mark the physical lever,
melody-preservation claim, artist fit or full creative gateway passed. Literal
outer preservation is not proof of complete melodic identity preservation.
