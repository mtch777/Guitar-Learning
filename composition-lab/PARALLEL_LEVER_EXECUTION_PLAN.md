- **QA1:** complete new-song source and coverage check. No opportunity is a valid coverage outcome but cannot close a positive creative gate.
- **QA2:** actual isolated inverse without source/lower/caches/expected payloads; complete exact 0 comparison plus actual supported nonzero through the same relations.
- **QA3:** independent relationship arithmetic, full proposal/support denominators, source provenance, meaningful contrasts and qualified interpretation. Computational conformity is distinct from human musical judgment.
- **Release:**0 exact;50 probabilistic release measured across opportunities/seeds;100 removes direct original preference and permits coincidence. Release, changed notation and actual audible change are separate counts.
- **Output:** GP/source-native/MIDI/audio locks and measured sound consequences where applicable. Preserve original previews, report codec limitations and human musical/physical acceptance separately.
- **END:** review, scope receipt, progress log and source-only commit/verified CI. A checkpoint is not a reason to stop authorized independent work; proceed to the next ready dependency without silently expanding scope.
