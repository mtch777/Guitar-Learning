and lowest pitch. It does not yet provide the harmonic/degree ownership needed
to release doubling or inversion while holding chord identity fixed. Its exact
context comparisons also provide sparse support. Prediction of permitted spacing
choices and a full musical voicing definition are distinct future tasks; neither
can be claimed from fingering-only success or interval sharing alone.
