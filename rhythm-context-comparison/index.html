Scoring rules

Exact scoring specification

Current phrase 40% · previous phrase 25% · preceding sequence 15% · riff context 20%. Missing evidence is omitted, not treated as a mismatch. Candidates are drawn proportionally to score, not selected by highest score.

Exact phrase length required. All source riffs with known opening role are eligible, including different known roles and the seed riff. No rank cap. Variation labels do not filter candidates.

Carry-in/out notes are preserved as source intervals, not inferred ties between newly paired phrases. Carry-in notes do not trigger a new click at the boundary.
