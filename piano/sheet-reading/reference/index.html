Staff Notes

Treble clef (bottom to top)

Lines: E4 G4 B4 D5 F5

Spaces: F4 A4 C5 E5

Bass clef (bottom to top)

Lines: G2 B2 D3 F3 A3

Spaces: A2 C3 E3 G3

Middle C and accidentals

Middle C is C4, on the ledger line below treble or above bass.

♯ raises a note by one semitone. ♭ lowers it by one semitone. ♮ cancels an accidental.
